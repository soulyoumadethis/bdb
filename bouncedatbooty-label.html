BounceDatBooty Records — Bass Music Label

BounceDatBooty Records is an independent record label from the underground. Our mission is to help artists reach wider audiences, maximize their creative potential, and push the boundaries of bass music.

We specialize in trap, jersey club, footwork, and everything that makes the speakers shake. No boundaries, just pure bass.
